Acceptance requires genuine verifyVc success plus exact review digest, issuer,
Seller, claims and finite expected dates. A successful human signature also runs
expired/tampered copies through the verifier and tests wrong expected subject
against the original valid signature. Full credential/proof stays in memory;
wallet, network, role or reload invalidates the session. A shared lease serializes
wallet connection, VC and SDK operations and remains held until a pending wallet
request settles. Rejection or stale replies cannot restore verification.

no full VC, signature, arbitrary errors or transaction identifiers.
